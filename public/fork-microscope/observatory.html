Each dot is the observed outcome proportion among saved continuations at that checkpoint. The dashed line is the saved reconstruction, including estimates between checkpoints. Shaded regions are candidate change intervals, not exact causal tokens or established decision forks.

Point bars are 95% Wilson intervals conditional on retained branches. They are individual intervals, not simultaneous confidence bands or a test that a change is real. Text matching measures recorded mentions, not correctness or hidden model state. A token cap can change the readout without changing the decision.

A candidate region is a lead to inspect, not a proven decision point.
