What do the answer labels measure?

The labels track literal answer text anywhere in a completed reply. They are not added to your prompt. Matching ignores case and repeated whitespace; multiple matches, missing answers, and unfinished replies count as Other. Mentions or rejected alternatives can match. This is not a correctness judgment.
